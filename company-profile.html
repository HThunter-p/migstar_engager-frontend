WHO WE ARE

A shared space for discovery and participation

MIGSTAR ENGAGER is a campaign platform built around public content, clear task steps and review points. Creators submit campaign ideas; eligible engagers can find approved tasks and respond to a prompt about the content.

Campaigns and payment requests go through administrative review. The platform is a prototype: available roles, levels, fees and campaign packages are configured by the administrator.

THE JOURNEY

From campaign idea to completed task

Here is what each person does, and where review fits into the process.

01 ◎

Choose your role

Register as a creator, an engager, or both. Role activation follows the instructions and approval steps shown in your account.

One account · A workspace for each role
02 ↗

Submit a campaign

Creators share a supported public content link, choose an objective and provide the campaign details. The campaign is sent for review.

Campaigns are not live until approved
03 ◷

Explore an eligible task

Active engagers browse available approved tasks, open the original content and follow the task timer before answering its question.

Tasks depend on availability and eligibility
04 ✓

Track activity and requests

Use the dashboard to see account status and request progress. Campaign and payment decisions are subject to administrative review.

Check your account for current status
MADE FOR BOTH SIDES

Two ways to take part

Start with the role that fits you. You can select both when you register.

FOR CREATORS

Share a campaign brief

Bring your public content and campaign objective to one place. Once your creator role is active, submit a campaign for review and manage it from your workspace.

  • Submit supported public content links
  • Choose an objective and campaign package
  • Follow review and payment request status

Creator activation fees, free campaign trials and package pricing are set by the administrator.

FOR ENGAGERS

Discover tasks with a clear next step

Choose an available level, request activation and browse eligible tasks when your engager account is active. Each task guides you to the original content and a short response.

  • See tasks available to your active account
  • Open the campaign content and follow the timer
  • Submit the requested answer and track its status

Engager levels and their terms are configured by the administrator and shown before activation.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

What review means here

Review and task steps help organize participation; they do not promise a particular result.

Campaign review

Campaign submissions are checked before they are made available to engagers.

Payment review

Payment instructions, submitted evidence and requests are handled through the account workflow.

Honest outcomes

A timer or task answer is not proof of genuine views, follows, likes, or other off-platform engagement.

MIGSTAR ENGAGER is a prototype. Fees, levels, package availability, approvals and withdrawal terms are administrator-configured and may change. No campaign outcome, engagement result or earnings are guaranteed. Follow the rules of the platform hosting the original content.
